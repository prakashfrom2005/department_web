About

Sree Saraswathi Thyagaraja College (STC), established in 1997, is located in Pollachi, Tamil Nadu. It is affiliated with Bharathiar University and offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs. The college is dedicated to providing quality education and holistic development, focusing on practical learning and innovation. With a commitment to community engagement and ethical values, STC aims to prepare students for real-world challenges and career success.

Faculty Details

The faculty at Sree Saraswathi Thyagaraja College (STC) is dedicated to providing quality education and fostering student success. The college boasts over 150 lecturers, with more than 70% holding doctorates, and many have extensive educational and industrial experience. The faculty-to-student ratio ranges between 35 to 50 students per teacher, ensuring a supportive learning environment. They are commended for their subject knowledge and dedication to student growth, offering additional support outside class hours
